Hi, welcome to *Web of Trust*, [Sam Hart](https://hxrts.com)'s workshop-style blog, containing ideas and sketches from my current experiments.

Since most of my work is digital, I'll be treating each post like a virtual studio visit. The degree of polish may vary, but each post should capture some kernel of interest.

I may touch on open source software projects, developed to work through various ideas, including [Aura](https://github.com/hxrts/aura), [Telltale](https://github.com/hxrts/aura), and [Jacquard](https://github.com/hxrts/jacquard).

The name "Web of Trust" owes to the concept of distributed key authentication proposed by Phil Zimmermann in 1992, as a solution to PGP's social scalability challenges. I've always been fond of the term because it frames a deeply technical system in human terms.
